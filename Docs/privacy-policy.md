# Marionette Privacy Policy

Marionette collects no data about you. The app is made by Turing Software, LLC ("we"), and this
policy covers the Marionette app for iPhone and iPad.

## What Marionette does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
- It has no analytics, advertising or tracking code, ours or any third party's.
- It does not ask you to make an account.
- It does not send anything to us: we run no server for the app.

## Data we collect and how we use it

None. What you save in the app (computers, passwords, SSH keys and settings) stays on your device,
and in your own Apple backups or iCloud Keychain only if you turn those on. Your sign-in, input,
clipboard and files go only to the computers you choose to connect to.

## Third parties

We share no data with any third party, because we have none to share.

## Retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete, and no consent to revoke.
You can delete anything you saved in the app, from within the app, at any time.
